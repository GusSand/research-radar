## 01 · [Deep Ignorance: Filtering Pretraining Data Builds Tamper-Resistant Safeguards into Open-Weight LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06601)

`pretraining-safety` `data-filtering` `tamper-resistance` — O'Brien et al. — arXiv preprint, Aug 2026

Post-training safety measures for open-weight LLMs can be erased in dozens of fine-tuning steps; this paper shows that filtering dual-use knowledge from the pretraining corpus itself produces models that resist adversarial fine-tuning by orders of magnitude longer.

![Figure 1: Overview of the Deep Ignorance pipeline — multi-stage topic classification and contamination filtering removing biothreat-relevant documents before pretraining](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.06601/x1.png)

*Figure 1: Multi-stage pretraining data filtering pipeline: topic classifiers and contamination heuristics identify and remove biothreat-relevant documents before model training begins.*

The authors pretrain multiple 6.9B-parameter models from scratch using a multi-stage pipeline that identifies and removes biothreat-relevant documents via topic classifiers and heuristic contamination filters. Resulting models score near-zero on biothreat proxy benchmarks and sustain that behavior under up to **10,000 steps and 300M tokens** of adversarial fine-tuning on biothreat-related text — exceeding post-training RLHF/DPO baselines by over an order of magnitude. Crucially, no degradation in unrelated capabilities (coding, math, general QA) was observed, addressing the key concern that data filtering trades safety for quality.

## 02 · [Synthetic Persona Pretraining: Alignment from Token Zero](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13482)

`pretraining-safety` `spec-driven` `synthetic-persona` — Julian Minder, Viktor Moskvoretskii, Raghav Singhal et al. (EPFL dlab) — arXiv preprint, Aug 13, 2026

This is the first rigorous test of spec-driven alignment injected at pretraining: annotating 10% of training documents with value-aligned moral reflections derived from a normative constitution reduces jailbreak attack success rate by 63% — but only if the intervention starts from the very beginning of training.

![Figure 1: Synthetic Persona Pretraining method — documents annotated with value-aligned first-person reflections, pretrained end-to-end, with persona binding in post-training](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13482/x1.png)

*Figure 1: SPP annotates pretraining documents with first-person constitutional reflections; the persona is then bound to the assistant role in post-training. Early injection (token zero) significantly outperforms late-stage injection.*

SPP annotates pretraining documents with first-person moral reflections derived from a normative value constitution, then pretrains via standard cross-entropy on both original and annotated text. Models trained to **3B parameters on 500B tokens** achieve **1.7% mean attack success rate** vs ~4.6% baseline — a **63% reduction**. The critical finding is temporal: alignment injected from token zero substantially outperforms late-stage (end-of-pretraining) SPP injection in both constitution adherence and choice alignment on out-of-distribution moral dilemmas, while preserving downstream capabilities.

## 03 · [Beyond Safe Data: Pretraining-Stage Alignment with Regular Safety Reflection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19168)

`pretraining-safety` `safety-reflection` — Jinhan Li, Kexian Tang, Yihan Xu, Zhuorui Ye, Kaifeng Lyu — arXiv preprint, Jun 17, 2026

Training only on safe data is insufficient: models can still combine benign knowledge unsafely. This paper inserts short self-reflective safety passages at regular intervals throughout the pretraining corpus, building a self-monitoring prior into the model foundation below post-training.

![Figure 1: Safety Reflection Pretraining — periodic safety reflection passages interleaved into standard pretraining corpus, showing improved safety classification vs baseline](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19168/x1.png)

*Figure 1: Regular safety reflections inserted into pretraining corpus improve safety classification accuracy and reduce attack success rates on 1.7B models pretrained on FineWeb-Edu.*

Safety Reflection Pretraining inserts short self-reflective safety passages at regular intervals in the pretraining corpus, training the model to reason about harm while learning language. Experiments with **1.7B models on FineWeb-Edu** show improved safety classification accuracy and substantially reduced success rates for both inference-stage jailbreaks and fine-tuning attacks. The approach is additive with standard post-training pipelines.
