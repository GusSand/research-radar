## Top 3 (priority order)

### 1. [**Model Spec Midtraining: Improving How Alignment Training Generalizes**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02087)
- **Authors / venue:** Chloe Li, Nevan Wichers, Sara Price, Samuel Marks, Jon Kutasov — Anthropic, arXiv preprint (May 2026); also published on [Anthropic Alignment Blog](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/msm/)
- **Why it ranks here:** Directly addresses midtraining-time safety intervention using model spec content; demonstrates dramatic reduction in agentic misalignment without touching post-training demonstrations
- **Technical summary:** Model Spec Midtraining (MSM) inserts a large synthetic corpus discussing Model Spec content (the *what* and *why* of desired behavior) as a midtraining stage between pretraining and alignment fine-tuning. The key idea is that demonstration data alone underspecifies desired generalization; exposing the model to explicit value reasoning narrows the hypothesis space before SFT. Evaluated on Qwen3-32B, MSM with a spec addressing self-preservation and goal-guarding reduces the agentic misalignment rate from 54% to 7%, beating a deliberative alignment baseline (14%). Crucially, the approach controls which values models acquire from identical AFT data by varying only the midtraining spec content.

![Figure 1: MSM pipeline showing pretraining → MSM (model-spec corpus) → AFT → aligned model, with agentic misalignment rates at each stage](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02087/x1.png)

*Figure 1: MSM pipeline. Adding a model-spec midtraining stage between pretraining and alignment fine-tuning controls which values are acquired from downstream demonstrations. Qwen3-32B misalignment drops from 54% (no MSM) to 7% (MSM), versus 14% for deliberative alignment.*

### 2. [**Constitutional Midtraining: Content Presence Drives Alignment Gains**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26654)
- **Authors / venue:** arXiv preprint (July 30, 2026)
- **Why it ranks here:** Largest-scale controlled test of constitutional midtraining (120B tokens); cleanly isolates midtraining from post-training and shows durable alignment gains, including against blackmail after SFT
- **Technical summary:** Builds a 394M-token constitutional corpus from Anthropic's Constitution and tests midtraining at 120B scale with a 2×2 factorial design (curriculum ordering × deliberative-reasoning presence) against a replay-only control. Evaluates on alignment-under-pressure benchmarks, value conflict resolution, blackmail elicitation, and emergent misalignment. Constitutionally midtrained models outperform controls on alignment generalization and durability; notably, constitutional midtraining blunts the blackmail propensity instilled by SFT, and this advantage survives subsequent benign fine-tuning. The title refers to a key finding: it is the presence of principled *content* in the corpus (not just the format or quantity) that drives gains.

![Figure 1: 2x2 factorial experimental design showing constitutional midtraining conditions and alignment scores across benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26654/x1.png)

*Figure 1: 2×2 factorial design. Constitutional midtraining at 120B scale with and without deliberative reasoning vs. replay-only control. Constitutionally midtrained models show durable safety advantages across all evaluation axes.*

### 3. [**Circuit Discovery Helps Detect LLM Jailbreaking: A Mechanistic Interpretability Study**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27504)
- **Authors / venue:** Paria Mehrbod, Boris Knyazev, Guy Wolf, Eugene Belilovsky, Geraldin Nanfack — ICML Workshop on Reliable and Responsible Foundation Models (workshop paper, August 2026)
- **Why it ranks here:** Applied mech-interp result that uses circuit discovery to operationally reduce jailbreak attack success; bridges circuit analysis directly to a safety-relevant outcome metric
- **Technical summary:** Using edge attribution patching and subnetwork probing on LLaMA-2-7B-chat, the authors identify specific attention heads and MLP pathways whose activity mediates affirmative responses to jailbreak prompts. Ablating these circuits *during first-token prediction only* reduces attack success rates by up to 80%, demonstrating their causal role in safety bypass. The analysis identifies how important tokens (e.g., trigger phrases) propagate through these components to override safety constraints, offering both an explanation of the mechanism and a targeted, computationally cheap intervention point.
