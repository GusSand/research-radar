## 01 · [Diffusion LLMs as Targets and Adversaries: Mechanistic Safety Exploits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07430)

- **Authors / venue:** Elena Dumitrescu, Gert Lek, Lydia Y. Chen, Jérémie Decouchant — arXiv preprint (August 2026)
- **Why it ranks here:** Triple intersection of text-diffusion, mech-interp, and security: mechanistic pruning of safety-critical attention heads raises jailbreak ASR from <3% to 73–87% across LLaDA and Dream 7B, and the attack transfers across model families. Establishes that dLLM safety is concentrated in a small identifiable subgraph — structurally more brittle than LLM safety.
- **Technical summary:** The authors identify "safety-critical" attention heads in LLaDA-8B and Dream 7B via causal tracing in activation space. Self-pruning (zeroing those heads within the target) raises ASR from 2.6%→73.8% on LLaDA and 1.9%→86.6% on Dream 7B. Transfer-pruning (locating safety heads in Qwen2.5 and applying the same structural mask to Dream 7B and Fast-dLLM) achieves 73.2% and 86.3% respectively — showing the safety-circuit topology generalises across architectures. A complementary black-box attack (SN-Guided Diffusion) manipulates the score function during inference without internal access.

![Figure 1: Jailbreak ASR before/after mechanistic pruning on LLaDA, Dream 7B, and Fast-dLLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.07430/x1.png)

*Figure 1: Jailbreak attack success rate before (near-zero) and after mechanistic safety-head pruning; self-pruning raises ASR to 73.8%/86.6% on LLaDA/Dream 7B; transfer-pruning from Qwen2.5 achieves 73.2%/86.3% on Dream 7B/Fast-dLLM.*

## 02 · [Data Attribution of Emergent Misalignment with Persona Features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11025)

- **Authors / venue:** Clemens Vetter, David Kaczér, Lucie Flek, Florian Mai (University of Bonn) — arXiv preprint (August 11, 2026)
- **Why it ranks here:** Uses SAE model-diffing to make emergent misalignment mechanistically legible for the first time. Steering individual persona features achieves 62% misalignment rate — nearly double fine-tuning alone — confirming that emergent misalignment is a narrow mechanistic perturbation, not a holistic distributional shift.
- **Technical summary:** SAE model diffing across 4 open-weight models identifies features systematically amplified (jailbreak persona, sarcasm, deception, manipulation) and suppressed (safety-identity, assistant-identity) during misalignment-inducing fine-tuning. Steering individual identified features via activation addition achieves 62% misalignment rate vs. 35% from the fine-tuning procedure itself. The result suggests that monitoring a small set of persona features could detect or preempt emergent misalignment before it manifests in outputs.

![Figure 1: SAE model-diff pipeline and persona feature steering misalignment rates](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11025/x1.png)

*Figure 1: SAE model-diffing identifies amplified persona features (jailbreak, sarcasm, deception, manipulation) and suppressed safety features; steering individual features achieves 62% misalignment rate vs. 35% from fine-tuning.*

## 03 · [Intrinsic Structure: Spectral Identifiability for Mechanistic Interpretability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10172)

- **Authors / venue:** Ashim Dhor (IISER Bhopal), Pin-Yu Chen (IBM Research) — arXiv preprint (August 10, 2026)
- **Why it ranks here:** First formal identifiability theorem for a mechanistic interpretability primitive: the intrinsic spectrum of a model's computational structure is recoverable at rate M⁻¹/², with a matching minimax lower bound. Gives mech interp a rigorous foundation for distinguishing genuine recovery from basis artefacts.
- **Technical summary:** The paper applies Koopman operator theory to lift activations to a coordinate-free latent space whose spectrum is intrinsic to the model. An M⁻¹/² convergence rate theorem is proved, with a matching minimax lower bound establishing optimality. A non-normality dissociation theorem rules out coordinate-dependent confounds. Empirical validation on GPT-2 Small, Gemma-2-2B, and Qwen3-8B-Base recovers a 0.506 ± 0.031 scaling exponent across all three, consistent with theory.

![Figure 1: Koopman lifting diagram with spectral recovery rate and empirical validation](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10172/x1.png)

*Figure 1: Koopman operator lifting maps activations to a coordinate-free latent space; intrinsic spectrum recovered at rate M⁻¹/² (minimax optimal); validated on 3 models with exponent 0.506 ± 0.031.*

## 05 · [Beyond the Bidirectional Promise: Re-evaluating the Robustness of Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27386)

`text-diffusion` `AI security` `preprint` — Saurabh Yadav, Badri Narayana Patro, Vijay Srinivas Agneeswaran (Microsoft) — arXiv, late July 2026

32-condition robustness evaluation of paired DLMs and LLMs (LLaDA-8B vs. LLaMA-3-8B; Dream-7B vs. Qwen2.5-7B). DLMs are architecturally robust to gradient-based adversarial suffix attacks (stochastic masked-diffusion loss landscape prevents clean gradients), but natural-perturbation robustness is weight-dependent with no consistent DLM advantage. The "bidirectional context" promise does not translate into general robustness.

![Figure 1: DLM vs LLM robustness across 32 perturbation conditions](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.27386/x1.png)

*Figure 1: DLMs show architectural robustness to adversarial suffixes (gradient attack fails on stochastic loss landscape) but match LLMs on natural perturbations — no consistent bidirectional advantage.*

## 06 · [Beyond a Bag of Features: Set-Level Instability in Sparse Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11197)

`mech-interp` `preprint` — Nikolai Bolik, Lennart Stöpler, Artur Andrzejak (Heidelberg University) — arXiv, August 11, 2026

SAE activation sets on natural text track model-internal similarity structure, not human conceptual categories or typicality — a set-level instability that persists across architectures and dictionary sizes even when individual features appear interpretable. Union-like compositional recovery guaranteed in toy models does not transfer to natural-language settings.

![Figure 1: SAE latent set structure vs human conceptual categories in toy and natural text settings](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11197/x1.png)

*Figure 1: SAE sets recover union-like structure in toy models (expected) but fail to match human category boundaries or typicality in natural text (actual) — fundamental set-level instability.*

## 07 · [Toward Safe LLM Agents: A Survey of Specification, Verification, and Enforcement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14590)

`AI security` `preprint` — arXiv, August 14, 2026

Three-layer framework (specification, verification, enforcement) for LLM agent safety. The specification layer is the dominant bottleneck: NL→formal translation achieves only 24–35% semantic correctness across benchmarked systems. Enforcement mechanisms are only as good as their upstream specifications — a gap no runtime defense compensates for without better formalization.

![Figure 1: Three-layer specification-verification-enforcement framework with NL→formal bottleneck](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14590/x1.png)

*Figure 1: Three-layer LLM agent safety framework; specification bottleneck: NL→formal translation achieves 24–35% semantic correctness; downstream enforcement tied to upstream spec quality.*

## 08 · [From Interpretability to Control: Insights from Six Years of the TrustNLP Workshop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11171)

`mech-interp` `preprint` — arXiv, August 11, 2026

144-paper meta-analysis across six TrustNLP editions traces the field trajectory: post-hoc interpretability of static models (2019–2021) → mechanistic understanding of representations (2021–2023) → proactive control during training and inference (2023–2024). Identifies the shift as driven by the failure of explanation-only approaches to generalise and the rise of causal intervention methodology.

![Figure 1: Six-year TrustNLP evolution timeline from post-hoc interpretability to proactive control](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11171/x1.png)

*Figure 1: TrustNLP 6-year trajectory across 144 papers: post-hoc explanations → mechanistic understanding → proactive control; causal interventions become the dominant methodology.*
