## 01 · [Xeno-Interpretability: Investigating the Alien Minds of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20408)

`mech-interp` `preprint` — F. Pierucci, M. Bracale Syrnikov, M. Prandi, M. Galisai, F. Giarrusso, P. Bisconti — arXiv, September 17, 2026

Introduces the concept of *xeno-representations* — internal representations in LLMs that are reproducibly locatable, geometrically characterizable, and causally manipulable, yet lack any adequate human conceptual description. The paper argues that the space of LLM-native distinctions is substantially larger than what finite human descriptions can cover, and proposes separating experimental identification from semantic interpretation: a feature can be validated as causally active and spatially located without being semantically labeled. This has direct safety implications: safety features that are functionally reliable but semantically alien may exist but be missed by human-interpretability audits.
