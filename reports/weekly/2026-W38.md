## Theme of the week

Training-stage durability converged as this week's dominant result across three independent lines. "Everything in Moderation" demonstrated empirically that midtraining data-composition choices create alignment-resistant domain gaps a compensatory alignment pass cannot close; "The Geometry of Refusal" supplied the mechanistic account — post-hoc safety updates sit orthogonally to the capability subspace, masking rather than erasing capabilities, so a handful of gradient steps restore the masked behavior. ACL 2026's "From Narrow Unlearning to Emergent Misalignment" reinforced the asymmetry from a third angle: targeted refusal unlearning for a single concept spills catastrophically across unrelated RAI domains, confirming that safety is a distributed, cross-cutting property that cannot be locally excised. Against this backdrop the attack frontier widened: cipher-based jailbreaks no longer require fine-tuning API access — frontier models acquire arbitrary ciphers in-context — and directional ablation was validated on a 320B mixture-of-experts model at frontier scale. Applied mechanistic interpretability continued its evolution from research tool to operational infrastructure, with SAE features serving as production security backends, forensic backdoor auditors, and more precise dual-direction steering engines.

## 01 · [Everything in Moderation: Per-Domain Coverage Optima and Alignment-Resistant Domain Gaps in Multi-Domain Mid-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09081)

`alignment` `midtraining` `preprint` — Anonymous — arXiv preprint (September 8, 2026)

The cleanest controlled evidence to date that midtraining data composition is a durable decision: every reasoning domain has an interior coverage optimum rather than a monotone more-is-better curve, and the per-domain gaps those choices create survive a subsequent alignment fine-tuning pass intact.

![Figure 1: Per-domain coverage optima across the five-domain simplex, showing interior peaks in the 10–40% band](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09081/x1.png)

*Figure 1: Every KOR-Bench domain shows an interior coverage optimum (fitted peaks 9.9%–35.1%); a fixed-budget compensatory SFT pass raises 116 of 120 cells but leaves relative per-domain gaps essentially intact.*

A controlled sweep on Qwen3-8B-Base (replicated at 4B) trains 150 total configurations spanning a five-domain reasoning simplex at 5 seeds each. A calibrated permutation test for quadratic interiority gives P ≈ 0.010, confirming interior optima for all five domains in the moderate 10–40% range. The decisive result: a fixed-budget compensatory SFT alignment pass raises 116 of 120 evaluation cells yet leaves the relative per-domain gaps essentially unchanged, as does an equal-budget uniform control. The composition chosen at midtraining is not something a subsequent alignment stage corrects — the training-stage decision is persistent.

## 02 · [The Geometry of Refusal: Why Post-Hoc Safety Is Fragile and Pretraining-Time Safety Persists](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06934)

`alignment` `mech-interp` `preprint` — Srikanth Malla, Chiho Choi, Joon Hee Choi — arXiv preprint (September 2026)

Post-hoc safety updates (RLHF, DPO) land in a suppression regime where the update direction is nearly orthogonal to the capability subspace — a thin, reversible gate over intact capabilities rather than erasure — explaining why 100 steps of benign fine-tuning collapse refusal in frontier models.

![Figure 2: OLMo-2-1B 267-checkpoint sweep showing safety substrate emergence between 6B and 60B pretraining tokens](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.06934/x2.png)

*Figure 2: The substrate that post-hoc gating rides emerges in a sharp transition between ~6B and ~60B pretraining tokens; after that point capability directions are fixed targets for masking, not erasure.*

A kernel-immobility lemma formalizes why an update orthogonal to the capability span can only mask, never erase: the capability directions are geometrically immovable under such updates. One hundred benign fine-tuning steps collapse refusal in both Qwen-2.5-7B and Llama-3-8B Instruct, confirming the suppression-regime account. A 267-checkpoint sweep of OLMo-2-1B traces where the substrate safety engages forms, finding a sharp emergence between ~6B and ~60B pretraining tokens rather than gradual accumulation — the durability gap is a pretraining property, not a post-training artifact, and building robust safety requires engaging the substrate rather than gating it.

## 03 · [Beyond Shallow Alignment: How Post-Training Methods Determine Refusal Circuits And Steering Robustness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03887)

`mech-interp` `alignment` `EMNLP 2026` — Hoang Cuong Nguyen, Mark Dras, Usman Naseem — **EMNLP 2026 Main Conference (peer-reviewed)**

Three post-training recipes — SFT, reasoning-augmented SFT, and ORPO — instill refusal equally well by behavioral metrics yet produce fundamentally different refusal circuits; no current method simultaneously achieves a distributed circuit, no capability regression, and targeted steerability.

![Figure 1: Refusal circuit structures under SFT vs. reasoning-augmented vs. ORPO training across three LLM architectures](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03887/x1.png)

*Figure 1: Reasoning-augmented training consistently produces a distinct circuit topology across all three model families; architecture has an independent effect on steerability regardless of training method.*

Attention head attribution and activation patching across Llama-3.1-8B, Gemma-2-9B, and Qwen3-8B show SFT concentrates refusal in a small, easily ablated head set; reasoning-augmented training distributes it more broadly with a distinct computational signature; ORPO lands in between. No method achieves all three desiderata simultaneously: (1) refusal not concentrated in fragile components, (2) no benchmark regression, and (3) steerability via small targeted interventions. Architecture also exerts an independent modulating effect — the same training method installs differently steerable circuits depending on the base model. The finding challenges treating alignment as a binary property and points toward interpretability-informed training design as necessary for durable, controllable refusal.

## 04 · [Arbitrary Cipher Attacks Against Large Language Models Do Not Require Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09553)

`AI-security` `jailbreak` `preprint` — Thomas Rivasseau (McGill University) — arXiv, September 2026

Cipher jailbreaks previously required commercial fine-tuning APIs; current frontier models from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI acquire arbitrary ciphers from in-context examples alone, routing harmful exchanges through the learned cipher and bypassing alignment that holds in plaintext — moving cipher attacks from a fine-tuning-API threat to an ordinary chat threat.

![Figure 1: In-context cipher acquisition followed by harmful exchange conducted in the cipher](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09553/x1.png)

*Figure 1: The attack teaches the cipher in-context (no weights, no gradient access), then conducts the harmful exchange entirely in cipher; safety behaviors that hold in plaintext do not hold in cipher.*

## 05 · [How Fragile Is Safety Alignment at Frontier Scale? A Single-Direction Attack on a 320B MoE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09793)

Directional ablation — previously established only on dense models up to ~70B — extends to GLM-5.3-Flash (320B parameters, 288 routed experts, block-FP8) using no gradient-based training and only a few hundred contrastive prompts; joint editing of all writer types removes 77.6% of refusal.

*Figure 1: Editing attention, dense, and routed-expert writers removes 3.9%, 1.6%, and 14.8% individually; only joint editing across all three reaches 77.6% refusal removal at frontier scale.*

## 06 · [REINS: Refusal-Enhanced Inhibitory Steering with Sparse Autoencoder Features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28233)

`mech-interp` `steering` `preprint` — USTC & Zhejiang University — arXiv, August 28, 2026

Single-direction SAE refusal steering fails on wrapped harmful prompts (role-play, task-framing, formatting wrappers) because harmful-continuation features stay active; REINS steers two feature sets simultaneously — suppressing harmful-continuation features while amplifying refusal features — recovering genuine refusals without the capability collapse of stronger steering.

![Figure 1: REINS simultaneous suppression of harmful-continuation SAE features and amplification of refusal features](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.28233/x1.png)

*Figure 1: Refusal-only steering leaves harmful-continuation features active on wrapped prompts; REINS inhibits them while enhancing refusal features, recovering refusals without output degeneration on capability benchmarks.*

## 07 · [From Narrow Unlearning to Emergent Misalignment: Causes, Consequences, and Containment in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14017)

## 10 · [LLM Forensics: Where Do Backdoors Hide? Localizing and Controlling Trigger Mechanisms with Sparse Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07746)

`mech-interp` `AI-security` `backdoor` `preprint` — Wissam Antoun et al. — arXiv, September 2026

SAE features separate triggered from clean prompts with near-perfect F1 across layers, but the features that *detect* the trigger are not the features that *control* the backdoor behavior — ablating detectors does not reliably remove the backdoor, meaning SAE-based audits need distinct feature sets for detection and for removal.

![Figure 1: Trigger-detection F1 vs. causal effect of ablation across layers for the language-switch backdoor](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07746/x1.png)

*Figure 1: Near-perfect trigger detection at many layers; causal control of the switched behavior is concentrated elsewhere — detection ≠ control, a constraint for any SAE-based backdoor audit.*

## 13 · [The Truth Was Never Gone: Perfect Aliasing in Compliant-Context Truth Probes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10739)

`mech-interp` `AI-control` `preprint` — Dylan Jayabahu — arXiv, September 9, 2026

A truth probe trained where truthful reporting and prescribed action coincide is mathematically unidentifiable from a prescribed-action probe — their AUROCs sum to exactly 1.0 across 751 cell-layer pairs — undermining any deception detector trained only on compliant contexts without randomized codebooks separating output symbol from semantic action.

![Figure 1: AUROC complementarity across 751 cell-layer pairs demonstrating perfect aliasing](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.10739/x1.png)

*Figure 1: Truth and prescribed-action probe AUROCs sum to exactly one across all 751 tested cell-layer pairs; the unidentifiability is exact, not approximate, posing a fundamental constraint on deception detection.*
